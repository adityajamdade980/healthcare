Understand Your Doshas

Ayurveda traditionally describes three Doshas.

🌬️

Vata

Associated traditionally with movement and qualities such as lightness, dryness and variability.

🔥

Pitta

Associated traditionally with transformation, metabolism and qualities such as heat and intensity.

🌊

Kapha

Associated traditionally with stability, structure and qualities such as heaviness and steadiness.
